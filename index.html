utm / what & why

what's a utm?

A UTM is a small tag added to a URL that tells our analytics tools where someone came from when they clicked. Tags look like ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social.

why it matters

Without UTMs, every visit on valpashotels.com is anonymous "direct traffic." We can't tell whether a LinkedIn post, newsletter, or an industry article drove the click. With UTMs, PostHog attributes every click back to the campaign that produced it, so we can see what's actually working.

the five tags

source*where the link lives. LinkedIn, newsletter, email.
medium*the channel type. social, email, cpc, referral.
campaign*the specific initiative. ihif-2026, newsletter-april-2026.
contentdifferentiates two links in the same campaign. hero-cta vs footer-cta.
termpaid-search keyword. skip unless tagging google or bing ads.

worked example

Sharing the new bedbug-awareness blog on LinkedIn:

https://valpashotels.com/blog/bedbug-awareness
  ?utm_source=linkedin
  &utm_medium=social
  &utm_campaign=bedbug-awareness

when to use it

Any external link you share that points to valpashotels.com or our app. Internal Slack threads, Notion docs, or anywhere that doesn't land on a marketing page can skip it.
